Sorts the Dealer's ships by type, then by price, cheapest first.

## What changes

Without the mod, the station's Dealer lists its ships in the game's own order. With the mod, ships of the same type
are next to each other, with the types in alphabetical order as the screen shows them (such as K37 TNTRL), and within
each type the cheapest ship comes first.

The Fleet list isn't changed.

## Works with other mods

Together with [ShipHighlightTypeAndPrice](ShipHighlightTypeAndPrice), the types are grouped and stand out. It also
works with [ShipCopyPaste](ShipCopyPaste).
