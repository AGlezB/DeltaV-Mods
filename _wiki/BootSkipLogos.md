The game opens straight on the title screen, where your saves are, without the logos first.

## What changes

Without the mod, every time you start the game it first shows about 22 seconds of logos (the studio, Godot, the Kurki
collective) and a photosensitivity warning. You can skip them by pressing Enter or Esc, or clicking. With the mod,
they are skipped for you, and the game goes straight to the title screen. If the title screen isn't ready yet, you
see the game's loading bar for a moment.

The very first time you start the game, before it has saved its settings, the logos and the warning still play once,
so you do see the warning.

The story intro of a new game isn't changed.
