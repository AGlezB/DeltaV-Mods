Your ship's Adrenaline booster starts every dive switched off.

## What changes

The Adrenaline booster is a piece of equipment that gives you a burst of bullet time when you fire it: time slows
down for a moment. Without the mod, it starts every dive switched on. With the mod, it starts switched off, so it
doesn't fire unless you want it to.

You can switch it on at any time during a dive: press **J** to open the Onboard Maintenance System, go to the
*Mechanic* tab, and switch the booster on there. It then stays the way you set it for the rest of the dive.

Only your own ship's booster is changed.

## Changes how the game plays

While the booster is switched off, it also holds back the bullet time you would get from your pilot and from the
smart camera, so you get little or none. That is how the game treats a booster that is switched off: the mod only
changes how it starts. Switch it on in the *Mechanic* tab to get that bullet time back.

If bullet time or the booster does something wrong, try again without the mod before you report a bug to the game's
developers.
