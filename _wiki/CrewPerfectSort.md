Sorts the crew lists: people for hire by salary, your crew by job, also when you pick who goes over to a derelict.

## What changes

Without the mod, the station's Crew screen lists people in the game's own order, and a derelict lists your crew in
the order you hired them. With the mod:

- **Hire:** the cheapest first. People with the same salary are sorted by job, in alphabetical order (Astrogator,
  Geologist, Mechanic, Pilot).
- **Your Crew:** sorted by job, and within each job the lowest salary first.
- **Sending someone to a derelict:** in a dive, when you meet a derelict (an abandoned ship), you can send one of your
  crew over in a space suit to restart it. The people you can pick are listed as on Your Crew.

People who are equal in both are sorted by name, so the order never shuffles. On the Hire tab, the keyboard or
controller starts on the first person of the sorted list.

## Works with other mods

It works with [CrewCheapHireNotifier](CrewCheapHireNotifier) and
[CrewHighlightJobAndSalary](CrewHighlightJobAndSalary).

Don't use it together with *CrewSortByJobAndSalary*, the older mod it replaces: both sort the same lists.
